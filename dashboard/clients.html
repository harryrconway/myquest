How to use MyQuest
1

Add your clients

Go to the Clients page and add each customer — name, phone, email, address.

2

Add their property

On the client's card, click Add Property. Fill in bedrooms, bathrooms, clean type, access notes and alarm code.

3

Create a quote

Click New Quote on the client's card. The calculator uses the property details and your rates from Settings to generate a price.

4

Add your staff

Go to the Staff page and add your team members — name, contact, role, and start date.

5

Build a roster

Go to the Roster page and schedule who is working which days.

6

Add jobs

Once a client confirms, click Add Job on their card. Assign the property, date, time, and which staff member is doing it.

7

Track on the day

The dashboard shows today's jobs at a glance — who's working, what properties, and completion status.
