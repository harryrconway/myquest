Standard Operating Procedures

Before the interview

Finding & screening candidates

From identifying a need to booking the interview.

01 When to hire
  • Workload exceeds capacity for 2+ consecutive weeks
  • Taking on a new recurring client that can't be covered by the current team
  • Existing staff are regularly working outside their preferred hours
02 Where to advertise
  • Seek and Indeed — paid, highest quality applicants
  • Gumtree Jobs and Facebook Jobs — free, high volume
  • Local community Facebook groups (Gold Coast / Tweed area)
  • Word of mouth from existing staff — offer a referral bonus
03 What to include in the job ad

Key details

  • Title: Cleaning Technician
  • Type: Casual/Part-time, ABN or employment
  • Location: Gold Coast / Tweed Heads area
  • Pay: competitive hourly rate — state range

Requirements

  • Own transport and valid driver's licence
  • Smartphone (for scheduling and communication)
  • Physically fit — active role
  • Mon–Fri availability

What we provide

  • All equipment and chemicals supplied
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Supportive team environment

How to apply

Ask for a short paragraph on why they'd be a good fit, plus their phone number. This filters for people who can communicate clearly.

04 Application screening checklist

Green lights — progress these

  • Australian work rights confirmed
  • Own car and driver's licence
  • Location within reasonable commute
  • Clear, professional communication in application
  • Relevant experience (preferred, not required)

Red flags — do not progress

  • No phone number provided
  • Multiple jobs in the last 12 months each lasting under 2 months
  • Vague application with no personal detail
  • Asks about minimum hours or pay before showing interest in the role
05 Initial phone screen (15 min)

Questions to ask

  • "Walk me through your current availability — days and hours."
  • "Do you have your own car and a valid licence?"
  • "Have you done commercial or domestic cleaning before?"
  • "Are you looking for employment or are you set up as an ABN contractor?"
  • "Any physical limitations we should know about?"

What to tell them

  • Early starts from 7am, physically active work
  • All equipment and chemicals supplied
  • Paid trial job before any commitment either way
  • Will follow up within 48 hours
The interview

Interview questions

Work through these in order. Watch for red flags throughout.

06 Opening
  • "Tell me a bit about yourself and what draws you to cleaning work."
  • "What do you know about MyQuest?"
07 Experience & skills
  • "Describe your cleaning experience — what types of spaces have you worked in?"
  • "What cleaning chemicals and equipment are you comfortable using?"
  • "Have you worked to a cleaning checklist or quality standard before?"
  • "How do you ensure you haven't missed anything in a space?"
08 Reliability & availability
  • "Walk me through a typical week — what other commitments do you have?"
  • "How do you handle it when you're running late to something?"
  • "Rate your punctuality 1–10 and tell me why."
  • "What situation would cause you to call in and not make a shift?"
09 Scenarios
  • "A client contacts you directly to book a job outside of MyQuest — what do you do?"
  • "You arrive at a site and realise you've forgotten an important piece of equipment. What do you do?"
  • "A client tells you they're unhappy with your clean. How do you respond?"
  • "You notice something is damaged at a site that wasn't broken when you arrived. What do you do?"
  • "You finish a job 30 minutes early. What do you do with the time?"
10 Culture & attitude
  • "What do you think makes someone genuinely great at this job?"
  • "How do you feel about working independently most of the time?"
  • "What matters most to you in a job — be honest."
  • "Describe a time you went above and beyond for someone."
11 Red flags to watch for
  • Speaks negatively about every previous employer
  • Vague or evasive about gaps or short tenures
  • Can't clearly explain their transport situation
  • Asks only about pay and hours, shows no curiosity about the work
  • Distracted, checking phone, or disengaged during the interview
  • Overly rigid about days/hours before understanding the role
After the offer

Onboarding

Documents to collect and what to expect in the first weeks.

12 Pre-employment requirements

All of the following must be completed before the first paid job:

  • Valid police check issued within 3 years — upload to staff profile
  • Proof of right to work in Australia (passport, visa, or birth certificate + citizenship)
  • ABN registration confirmation (if contractor arrangement)
  • Driver's licence sighted and recorded
  • Signed contractor or employment agreement
13 Trial and probationary period
  • First shift is a paid trial alongside an existing staff member
  • Assess: punctuality, attention to detail, physical pace, attitude
  • Formal review at 4 weeks of regular work

Criteria to pass probation

  • Consistent punctuality
  • No client complaints
  • Follows checklists and instructions without prompting
