Around your trading hours, not ours.

We fit your cleaning schedule around when your space needs to be at its best: before customers arrive, not after they've already seen it.

  • Timing

    Before open, after close, or quiet midweek windows that don't disrupt trade. We confirm a regular time and stick to it.

  • Same cleaner

    Where possible, we send the same person each visit. They'll know your layout, your display standards and what matters.

  • Keys and alarms

    Access is arranged in writing before the first clean. Keys and alarm codes are handled securely; arrangements agreed before we start.

  • Frequency

    Daily, weekly, fortnightly or a once off for events and deep cleans. We'll recommend what actually makes sense for your foot traffic. Not sure which you need? Our regular clean or deep clean guide explains the difference. Running a gym or fitness studio? See our gym and studio cleaning.

Things retail managers ask us.

Can't see yours? Call Harry on 0413 154 932.

Can you clean outside trading hours?
Yes. Early mornings before you open, evenings after close, or quiet midweek windows. Most of our retail clients prefer us there before the first customer. We confirm a regular time and stick to it so there are no surprises.
Do you clean at the Gold Coast Airport?
Yes. We clean retail tenancies, airline offices, lounges and other commercial facilities at the Gold Coast Airport. Talk to us about your specific space, access requirements and any security processes. We'll work through them before the first clean.
Is it the same cleaner each visit?
Yes, where possible. Retail spaces benefit from someone who knows the layout, the display standards and the things that matter to you. If your regular cleaner is unavailable, we'll let you know in advance and send a vetted replacement.
Can you handle multiple tenancies or a retail strip?
Yes. If you manage a retail strip, a centre tenancy mix or multiple locations in a precinct, talk to us. We can schedule across them efficiently and give you a single point of contact for all of it.
Do you supply consumables like toilet paper and soap?
We bring all cleaning products and equipment. Consumables such as toilet paper, hand soap and paper towels are supplied by you. If you'd like us to manage restocking, talk to us and we can factor that into your arrangement.
What if something isn't right after a clean?
Let us know within 24 hours and we'll fix it, no questions asked. We'd rather come back and make it right than have you quietly unhappy. That's how the relationship stays worth having for both sides.
