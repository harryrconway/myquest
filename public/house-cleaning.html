Common questions

Things homeowners ask us.

Do I need to be home while you clean?
No. Plenty of our clients are at work while we clean. We agree access before the first visit, whether that's a spare key, a key safe or you letting us in, and handle it securely.
What's the difference between a regular clean and a deep clean?
A regular clean keeps your home on top of itself: kitchen, bathrooms, floors and dusting on a set schedule. A deep clean is a one off, top to bottom job that also covers inside the oven and fridge, cupboard fronts, skirting boards, window tracks and light fittings.
Is it the same cleaner each visit?
Yes, where possible. We aim to send the same person each time so they learn your home and how you like things done. If they're unavailable, we'll let you know in advance and send a vetted replacement.
Do you bring your own products and equipment?
Yes. We bring everything we need. If you'd prefer we use particular products in your home, just let us know.
How does pricing work?
It depends on the size of your home, the number of bathrooms, the type of clean and how often. The quote form gives you an instant estimate, then we confirm a fixed written price before we start. What we quote is what you pay.
What if something isn't right after a clean?
Let us know and we'll come back and fix it. We'd rather make it right than have you quietly unhappy.
