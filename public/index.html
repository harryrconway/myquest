The standard

What "done properly" actually means.

Anyone can clean where people are looking. Our standard lives in the places a quick once over misses: the edges, the corners, the details you only notice when they are wrong. It is the difference between a space that looks clean and one that feels genuinely cared for.

  • Appropriate insurance, in place before we arrive.
  • Police checked and personally interviewed, no exceptions.
  • A clear written price before any work begins.
  • Quality over quantity, every single visit.

Questions

Asked, answered.

Which areas does MyQuest service?
The Gold Coast from Surfers Paradise south to Coolangatta, including Broadbeach, Mermaid Beach, Miami, Burleigh Heads, Palm Beach, Currumbin and Tugun. The Northern Rivers, including Tweed Heads, Banora Point, Kingscliff, Casuarina, Cabarita Beach and Murwillumbah. Nearby but unsure? Ask and we'll tell you straight.
Do you take on once off cleans, or only ongoing contracts?
Both. Once off cleans and standing daily, weekly or fortnightly arrangements, shaped around your premises and your hours.
Are you insured?
Yes. We hold appropriate insurance for the services we provide, in place before we step through the door.
Who will be in our workplace?
Every team member is police checked and personally vetted before joining MyQuest. Your workplace, your people and your trust: we treat all three seriously.
How does pricing work?
You receive a clear written proposal before any work begins. No hidden fees, no surprise charges. The price we quote is the price you pay.
Can you work outside business hours?
For commercial clients, yes: early mornings, evenings and weekends where available, scheduled around your business rather than ours.
Do you clean medical centres?
Yes. GP clinics, dental practices, physio and allied health, and specialist rooms. Infection control focused, colour coded cloths and mops, after hours scheduling. Learn more about our medical centre cleaning →
Do you clean airports and retail spaces?
Yes. We clean retail floors, showrooms, studios and airport facilities including the Gold Coast Airport. Learn more about retail cleaning →
Do you clean homes?
Yes. We offer regular weekly, fortnightly or monthly house cleaning and one off deep cleans across the Gold Coast and Tweed. Learn more about house cleaning →
Do you clean for NDIS participants and DVA clients?
Yes. We clean homes for NDIS participants, veterans and DVA clients, with regular or deep cleans and clear invoices. Learn more about NDIS and DVA cleaning →
