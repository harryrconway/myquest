What's included

Builder's clean checklist.

  • Debris removal
    Collect and dispose of leftover materials, packaging and construction waste
  • Dusting and vacuuming
    Fine dust from floors, walls, ceilings, vents and skirting boards using HEPA-filter vacuums
  • Surface cleaning
    Cupboards, benchtops, cabinets and internal fixtures; paint splashes, adhesive and dust removed
  • Bathroom and kitchen cleaning
    Deep clean of sinks, taps, showers, tiles and appliances; grout haze and plaster dust removed
  • Window and glass cleaning
    Protective films, stickers and dust from all windows, mirrors and glass surfaces
  • Floor care
    Sweep, vacuum and mop all floors including tile, timber and laminate; construction marks removed
  • Polishing and finishing touches
    Surfaces buffed and polished where needed for a handover-ready finish

Questions

Builder's clean FAQ.

How long does a builder's clean take?
It depends on the size and condition of the site. A typical residential new build takes 4–8 hours; a larger commercial fit-out may take a full day or run across two days. We'll confirm timing when we give you a quote.
Do you remove construction waste and debris?
Yes. Debris removal is part of our standard builder's clean — we collect and dispose of leftover materials, packaging and construction waste. If you have unusually large volumes or specific disposal requirements, let us know and we'll account for that in the quote.
Can you work around other trades still on site?
Yes. We're experienced working alongside electricians, tilers, painters and other finishing trades. We'll co-ordinate timing with your site manager to avoid clashes.
Do you supply all your own equipment and products?
Yes. We bring everything — HEPA-filter vacuums, commercial cleaning products, cloths and equipment. You don't need to supply anything.
Can you do a staged clean across multiple visits?
Yes. For larger projects we can clean in stages as each area is completed. Talk to us about your project timeline and we'll work out a schedule that suits.
