Can NDIS funding pay for house cleaning?

Short answer: Often, yes. Many NDIS plans include help with household tasks such as cleaning. Whether it is in your plan depends on what has been approved for you, so check your plan, or ask your plan manager or support coordinator.

Updated November 20262 min readBy the MyQuest team

The support item cleaning falls under

For NDIS plans, cleaning is usually funded under House Cleaning and Other Household Activities (01_020_0120_1_1). It has an hourly price limit set by the NDIA, which you can find in the NDIS pricing arrangements and price limits.

How to check your plan

  • Look through your plan for help with household tasks or cleaning
  • Ask your plan manager or support coordinator whether cleaning is covered, and how many hours your budget allows
  • Tell us how your plan is managed, and we will talk through how we can work with it

If you are not sure where to start, call us on 0413 154 932. We are happy to talk it through with you, a family member or your coordinator.

What a clean covers

  • Kitchen. Benchtops, splashback, sink, stovetop and appliance fronts, with the microwave cleaned inside and bins emptied
  • Bathrooms. Showers, baths, toilets, basins, mirrors and taps
  • Floors. Vacuumed and mopped throughout
  • Dusting. Surfaces, shelves, skirting boards, light switches and door handles
  • Bedrooms and living areas. Surfaces wiped and rooms left neat

A deep clean adds the inside of the oven and fridge, cupboard fronts, window tracks, ceiling fans and light fittings. Our regular clean or deep clean guide explains which one suits.

Agreeing the hours

NDIS cleaning is charged by the hour. We agree the hours and the rate with you before the first clean, based on your home and the help you need, so you know how it fits your budget before anything is booked.

Invoices set out for claiming

Every invoice shows the support item, the dates and the hours, ready for you or your plan manager to claim. There are no surprise charges after a clean.

Who can arrange it

Anyone organising the cleaning can contact us, including family members, carers and support coordinators. We keep everyone who needs to know in the loop, and where possible we send the same cleaner each visit, so there is a familiar face at the door.

Veterans and DVA clients

For veterans, DVA may fund cleaning through Veterans’ Home Care, or pay towards it through Household Services, depending on your eligibility. Tell us how your support is set up and we will work with it. Read more on our NDIS and DVA cleaning page.
