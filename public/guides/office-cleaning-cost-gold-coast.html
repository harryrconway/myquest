How much does office cleaning cost on the Gold Coast?

Short answer: A regular clean of a small office, under 100 square metres, is estimated at about $175 to $210 per visit, including GST, and the price rises in steps with the size of your space. A deep clean costs more again, by an amount that depends on your office size.

Updated November 20262 min readBy the MyQuest team

What changes the price

Office size

Your office's approximate size sets the base time for a clean. Larger spaces take longer to cover, so the price rises in steps as size increases.

Kitchen or breakroom

If your office has a kitchen or breakroom, we add time to the visit for benchtops, the sink, appliances and bins.

Bathrooms

Every bathroom beyond the first adds more time. More bathrooms means more time spent on toilets, basins and floors.

Regular or deep

A regular clean keeps the office on top of itself. A deep clean adds a thorough reset through the space, so it costs more, with the exact difference depending on the size of your office rather than a flat percentage. Read regular clean or deep clean to see which one you need.

How often you book

Daily, weekly, fortnightly or monthly cleaning changes how often you're billed, not the price of each visit. A weekly clean and a fortnightly clean of the same office cost the same per visit; you're simply booking it more or less often.

Access on the day

If we can't get in at the booked time, the lockout and late cancellation fees in our policies can apply. Agreeing access before the first clean avoids them.

Why our prices include GST

Australian consumer law requires businesses to show households the total price, including GST. Many businesses quote each other prices before GST, as the norm, but we show every price the same way, home or business, so what we quote is what you pay, with nothing added on later.

What every office clean covers

  • Desks and touchpoints: surfaces wiped, phones, light switches and door handles sanitised
  • Kitchen and breakroom: benchtops, sink, appliances and bins, when included
  • Bathrooms: toilets, basins, mirrors, taps and dispensers cleaned and restocked
  • Vacuuming and mopping: all floor types, under desks and along edges
  • Meeting rooms and glass: tables, chairs, boards and internal glass wiped and reset
