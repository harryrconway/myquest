Common questions

Things people ask us.

Can I use NDIS funding for cleaning?
Many NDIS plans include help with household tasks such as cleaning. Check your plan, or ask your plan manager or support coordinator, then talk to us and we'll work through the details with you.
Do you work with veterans and DVA clients?
Yes. We clean for veterans, war widows and widowers, and their families. Tell us about the support you receive and we'll work out an arrangement that suits you.
Can a family member or support coordinator arrange it for me?
Yes. Whoever is organising the cleaning can contact us, and we'll keep everyone who needs to know in the loop.
Is it the same cleaner each visit?
Yes, where possible. A familiar face matters, so we aim to send the same person each time. If they're unavailable, we'll let you know in advance and send a vetted replacement.
Do I need to be home while you clean?
No, but you're welcome to be. Some clients like us to clean while they're out, others prefer to be there. We agree access before the first visit and handle keys securely.
How is it priced?
We agree a price with you before the first clean, based on your home and the help you need, and send clear, itemised invoices. There are no surprise charges.
