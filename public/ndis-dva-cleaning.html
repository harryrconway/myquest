Things people ask us.

Can I use NDIS funding for cleaning?
Many NDIS plans include help with household tasks such as cleaning. Check your plan, or ask your plan manager or support coordinator, then talk to us and we'll work through the details with you. Our guide to NDIS funding for house cleaning explains how it works.
Do you work with veterans and DVA clients?
Yes. We clean for veterans, war widows and widowers, and their families. Tell us about the support you receive and we'll work out an arrangement that suits you.
Can a family member or support coordinator arrange it for me?
Yes. Whoever is organising the cleaning can contact us, and we'll keep everyone who needs to know in the loop.
Is it the same cleaner each visit?
Yes, where possible. A familiar face matters, so we aim to send the same person each time. If they're unavailable, we'll let you know in advance and send a vetted replacement.
Do I need to be home while you clean?
No, but you're welcome to be. Some clients like us to clean while they're out, others prefer to be there. We agree access before the first visit and handle keys securely.
How is it priced?
We agree the hours and price with you before the first clean, based on your home and the help you need, and send clear, itemised invoices with no surprise charges. For NDIS plans, cleaning is usually funded under House Cleaning and Other Household Activities (01_020_0120_1_1), which has an hourly price limit set by the NDIA, and we can show the support item, dates and hours on every invoice. For veterans, DVA may fund cleaning through Veterans’ Home Care or pay towards it through Household Services, depending on your eligibility. Our house cleaning price guide shows roughly how many hours a visit takes by home size, which can help when you're budgeting hours against your plan.
