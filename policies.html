Our Policies

Transparency and trust are at the heart of everything we do.

Last updated: June 2026

Please review the following policies before engaging MyQuest Cleaning Services. These terms help ensure a smooth and professional experience for both our clients and our team.

Lockout Policy

We value your time and ask that you respect ours. To ensure your scheduled clean proceeds without disruption, please ensure one of the following arrangements is in place prior to our arrival:

  • Leave keys or an access card in an agreed-upon location
  • Arrange for a site representative to be present to provide entry
  • Provide building access codes or security details in advance

An SMS reminder will be sent to you 24 hours before your scheduled appointment.

If our team cannot access the premises

We will attempt to contact you within 15 minutes of arrival. If we are unable to reach you and access cannot be arranged, a full lockout fee will apply.

  • Entry arranged within 30 minutes: A $25 fee will apply to compensate our team for lost time.
  • Full lockout fee: 75% of the scheduled service cost, up to a maximum of $150. This covers travel time, lost income, and scheduling disruption.

Late Cancellation Policy

We require a minimum of 24 hours' notice for all cancellations or reschedules.

  • Late cancellation fee: Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice will incur a fee of 50% of the scheduled service cost, up to a maximum of $150.

We understand that unexpected situations arise. Genuine emergencies will be assessed on a case-by-case basis. However, repeated late cancellations may result in termination of ongoing services.

To cancel or reschedule, please contact us as early as possible by phone on 0413 154 932 or email cleaning@myquest.au.

Payment Policy

One-off and initial cleans

  • Full payment is due the day before the scheduled service, or
  • A 50% deposit is required before the clean, with the remaining balance due on the day of service

Regular ongoing contracts

  • Payment is due upon receipt of invoice, unless otherwise agreed in writing

Accepted payment methods

  • Bank transfer (EFT)
  • Credit card
  • Debit card

Late payments

An interest-based late fee of 10% of the outstanding balance per month will apply to overdue invoices. Please ensure timely payment to avoid additional charges.

Privacy Policy

We collect your name, contact details, site address, and service preferences solely for the purpose of scheduling and delivering your cleaning services. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties except as required to deliver our services or comply with legal obligations.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Terms of Service

By engaging MyQuest Cleaning Services, you agree to the following terms:

Site access and preparation

Clients are responsible for ensuring safe and clear access to all areas included in the scope of service. Please ensure relevant areas are accessible and that our team can work without obstruction.

Satisfaction guarantee

If you are not satisfied with any aspect of a clean, please contact us within 24 hours of the service. We will arrange a complimentary re-clean of the affected areas or offer a partial refund where appropriate.

Liability

MyQuest is fully insured. In the event that you believe damage has resulted from our cleaning, please notify us within a reasonable timeframe. Legitimate claims caused by negligence will be addressed through our insurance coverage. Standard wear and tear is not covered.

Right to refuse service

We reserve the right to decline or discontinue service in cases of unsafe working conditions, harassment of our staff, or repeated policy violations.

Damage Policy

Reporting damage

Assessment and resolution
