Questions

Asked, answered.

Which areas does MyQuest service?
The Gold Coast from Surfers Paradise south to Coolangatta, including Broadbeach, Mermaid Beach, Miami, Burleigh Heads, Palm Beach, Currumbin and Tugun. The Northern Rivers, including Tweed Heads, Banora Point, Kingscliff, Casuarina, Cabarita Beach and Murwillumbah. And Adelaide's inner suburbs, including North Adelaide, Norwood, Unley, Burnside, Walkerville, Hyde Park and Glenelg. Nearby but unsure? Ask and we'll tell you straight.
Do you take on once off cleans, or only ongoing contracts?
Both. Once off cleans and standing daily, weekly or fortnightly arrangements, shaped around your premises and your hours.
Are you insured?
Yes. We hold appropriate insurance for the services we provide, in place before we step through the door.
Who will be in our workplace?
Every team member is police checked and personally vetted before joining MyQuest. Your workplace, your people and your trust: we treat all three seriously.
How does pricing work?
You receive a clear written proposal before any work begins. No hidden fees, no surprise charges. The price we quote is the price you pay.
Can you work outside business hours?
For commercial clients, yes: early mornings, evenings and weekends where available, scheduled around your business rather than ours.
