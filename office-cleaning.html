Who it's for

Offices, firms and workplaces of all kinds.

If your team works there, we'll keep it right. We clean offices for:

  • Corporate offices and professional firms — accountants, lawyers, real estate agents, financial advisers
  • Co-working spaces and shared suites — common areas, private offices, meeting rooms
  • Small and medium offices — anything from a single-tenancy suite to a full floor
  • Airport and airport facilities — including the Gold Coast Airport terminal and precinct businesses

We clean commercial spaces only. That focus is what keeps the standard consistent.

What's included

What every office clean covers.

  • Desks and touchpoints — surfaces wiped, phones, light switches, door handles and handles sanitised
  • Kitchen and breakroom — benchtops, sink, appliances, microwave interior; bins emptied and relined
  • Bathrooms — toilets, basins, mirrors, taps and dispensers cleaned and restocked where you supply consumables
  • Vacuuming and mopping — all floor types; under desks and along edges, not just the open areas
  • Meeting rooms — tables, chairs, boards and AV equipment wiped down and reset
  • Glass and partitions — internal glass cleaned to streak-free; windows where accessible
  • Bins — emptied and relined throughout; recycling separated where you have it set up
How scheduling works

Around your hours, not ours.

We fit your cleaning schedule to your business — not the other way around.

Timing

Early mornings before your team arrives, evenings after they leave, or weekends — whatever suits your office. We confirm a regular time and stick to it.

Same cleaner

Where possible, we send the same person each visit. You know who's in your space and they know your office. No surprises.

Keys and alarms

We handle access properly. Keys and alarm codes are managed securely; access arrangements are agreed in writing before the first clean.

Frequency

Daily, weekly, fortnightly or a once-off. We'll recommend what actually makes sense for your space — not just the most frequent option.

Office manager questions

Things people always ask us.

How do you handle keys and alarm codes?
Access arrangements are discussed and agreed in writing before the first clean. Keys are managed securely and alarm codes are handled with the same care. If you change your codes, just let us know and we update our records — no drama.
Is it the same cleaner each visit?
Yes, where possible. We aim to send the same person to your office each time so they learn your space and your preferences. If your regular cleaner is unavailable, we'll let you know in advance and send a vetted replacement.
Do you supply consumables like toilet paper and soap?
We bring all cleaning products and equipment. Consumables — toilet paper, hand soap, paper towels — are supplied by you. If you'd like us to manage restocking, talk to us and we can factor that into your arrangement.
What if something isn't right after a clean?
Let us know and we'll fix it — no questions asked. We'd rather come back and make it right than have you quietly unhappy. That's how the relationship stays worth having for both sides.
Do you offer daily cleaning?
Yes. Daily cleans are available for offices that need a consistent standard every morning. We'll confirm timing and scope based on your office size and what actually needs doing each day.
Can you clean outside business hours?
Yes. Early mornings, evenings and weekends. Most of our office clients prefer us there before the team arrives — we fit around your schedule rather than ours.
