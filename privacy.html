Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

MyQuest Cleaning Services (“MyQuest”, “we”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide when you contact us or book a service, including your name, phone number, email address, and service address. We do not collect sensitive information.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry or quote request
  • To schedule and manage your cleaning service
  • To send service reminders or follow-up communications
  • To comply with our legal obligations

Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share information with service contractors who assist in delivering your clean, and only to the extent necessary.

Cookies & Website Data

Our website does not use tracking cookies. We use Google Fonts, which may set a browser cache entry when the font files are downloaded. No analytics or advertising cookies are placed on your device.

Data Storage & Security

Information submitted through our contact form is transmitted securely. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at hello@myquest.au. We will respond within 30 days.

Contact

For any privacy-related enquiries, contact us at hello@myquest.au or call 0497 828 838.
