Who it's for

Clinics and health practices that need it done right.

We clean medical and allied health facilities across the Gold Coast and Tweed, including:

  • GP clinics and general practice — consultation rooms, waiting areas, reception and bathrooms
  • Dental practices — waiting rooms, patient areas, staff areas and bathrooms
  • Physiotherapy and allied health — treatment rooms, reception and common areas
  • Specialist rooms and consulting suites — dermatology, psychology, specialist medical
  • Pharmacies and health retail — retail floor, dispensary surrounds and staff areas

We focus on environmental cleaning — the areas your patients and staff move through every day. Clinical instrument cleaning and sharps handling remain with the practice and its licensed contractors.

Our process

What medical centre cleaning actually covers.

  • Consultation and treatment rooms — surfaces, examination tables (surrounds), benches and touchpoints cleaned with hospital-grade disinfectant
  • Waiting areas — chairs, tables, reception desk, door handles, light switches and high-touch surfaces
  • Bathrooms — deep clean of toilets, basins, taps, mirrors and dispensers; infection-control standard
  • Colour-coded cloths and mops — separate equipment for different zones to prevent cross-contamination
  • TGA-listed disinfectants — hospital-grade products used throughout clinical areas
  • Floors — vacuumed and mopped, including under furniture and along edges where dust accumulates
What we don't cover: Clinical instrument sterilisation, sharps and clinical waste disposal, and reprocessing of medical devices remain with the practice and its licensed clinical contractors. Environmental cleaning is our scope.

Questions

Medical centre cleaning FAQ.

Do you use hospital-grade disinfectants?
Yes. We use TGA-listed hospital-grade disinfectants in clinical areas — consultation rooms, treatment rooms, bathrooms and high-touch surfaces. We'll confirm the specific products on request if your practice has particular requirements.
Can you work after clinic hours?
Yes. Most of our medical centre clients prefer evening cleans after the last appointment, or early morning before the first. We schedule around your clinic hours, not ours. A midday touchpoint clean for high-volume practices is also available where the schedule allows.
Do you provide cleaning logs for accreditation purposes?
Yes. If your practice is working through RACGP accreditation or similar, we can provide signed cleaning logs and documented cleaning schedules. Let us know at the start and we'll make this part of your regular arrangement.
Do you use colour-coded cloths and mops?
Yes. We use colour-coded cloths and mops to prevent cross-contamination between zones — separate equipment for clinical areas, bathrooms and common areas.
What's not included in your medical centre cleaning?
Environmental cleaning is our scope. Clinical instrument sterilisation, reprocessing of medical devices, sharps removal and clinical waste disposal remain with the practice and its licensed clinical contractors. If you're unsure where the boundary sits for your practice, we're happy to discuss it upfront.
How do you handle patient privacy?
Our team understands they're working in spaces where patient records and sensitive information are present. We treat discretion as a baseline expectation. If your practice has specific protocols — paperwork face-down, particular areas off-limits — tell us and we'll observe them.
